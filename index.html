Your Data, Your Privacy

Important information about how LifeLedger currently handles your data.

Local Data Storage

Your main LifeLedger records, including routine, trading and finance information, are stored locally on your Windows device.

Google Sign-In

Google sign-in is provided through Supabase authentication. Google and Supabase privacy policies also apply when you use this feature.

Backup & Restore

LifeLedger provides local backup and restore options. Automatic cloud synchronization and cross-device data sync are not currently available in this release.

Payments

LifeLedger does not require an in-app subscription for the features included in this release.
